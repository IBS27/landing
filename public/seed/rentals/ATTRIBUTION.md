# Rental reference media

These images come from the property-specific Realvision galleries linked in `convex/seed/rentalMedia.json`. Each catalog entry records the publishing listing URL, original photo and floor-plan URLs, and retrieval date. Photos and plans belong to their original publishers. Floor plans are the publisher's approximate drawings and have not been synthesized or redrawn.

Landing uses these units as demo references. Seller identities, prices, availability and private-room offers in the app are fictional and are not offers from the source agents.
