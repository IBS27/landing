# Seed image attribution

Images in `public/seed/` are used as fictional seed data. Each file below lists its
source, creator and license. Openverse images are Creative Commons or public-domain
licensed and allow commercial use and modification; they were resized and re-encoded
(EXIF stripped, and `pages/` center-cropped to square). Avatars come from
[randomuser.me](https://randomuser.me/), which provides its portraits free to use.
Resized copies of CC BY-SA images remain under the same CC BY-SA license.
Machine-readable data lives in `manifest.json`.
