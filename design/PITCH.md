In-person pitch. The demo is fully live; the deck covers only the intro and the close.

## Intro · 0:00–0:40 · A

*[Slide: Landing logo]*

> Every year, young adults move to new cities for a first job, an internship, or grad school. And for most of them, it's the first time they've done it alone: no dorm, no orientation week, no roommate assigned to them.
>
> They're picking an apartment from a thousand miles away in neighborhoods they've never seen. And when they finally get there, they don't know anyone.
>
> So we built **Landing**. It helps young adults move to a new city, from finding an apartment to finding their people, right inside Facebook.
>
> *[Click: Jordan's photo]* Meet Jordan. He's 22, just graduated from Georgia Tech, and just got the job as a new grad SWE at **Meta**. He starts in San Francisco in six weeks. He's never been here, no car, he's got a $2,400 budget, and he doesn't know a single person in this city.
>
> *[Turns to B]* Let's help Jordan land.

### 5. Living there · 3:35–4:35

*[C: opens Journeys.]*

> Moving is a one-time thing. Acclimating is a process. Jordan's moved in, and the first few months decide whether a city sticks, so Landing stays with him.
>
> Every week, Jordan gets a new Weekend Route built from real Events and his Groups' plans. This Saturday: Run Club, Golden Gate Park, 8am, and Dev and Priya are going. *[C: clicks "Join."]* **Muse drafts a quick "Mind if I join you?" to them, ready for him to send.**
>
> *[C: opens the crew chat. The "Plans from your chats" card shows "we should hike sometime."]*
> And this is where Muse really earns its place. Someone in the crew chat says "we should hike sometime."
>
> *[C: clicks "Plan it."]*
> **Muse reads the conversation: who floated it, who's in, and what everyone's into. It picks a trail that fits the group, maps a route there, and suggests times, all with sources.** *[Poll draft appears.]* **Then it drafts a poll for the crew.**
>
> **When the poll closes, Muse turns the winning time into a real Facebook Event, and on the day, it sends everyone a reminder.** Jordan approves each step, and "we should hike sometime" becomes Saturday at 9.
>
> Week by week, those plans turn into a routine. And before you know it, San Francisco feels like home.

- Jordan is logged in, the browser is zoomed to 125–150%, and the demo is open full screen in its own window.
- D has the backup recording of the full demo ready to play.

## Q&A prep (D leads)

- **"What if Muse is wrong?"** Every recommendation cites its sources, code checks that each citation is a source Muse was actually given, and nothing is sent without the user's approval.
- **"What's AI and what's code?"** Muse handles judgment: reading Group threads, profiles, intros, plans, the 3D reconstruction. Code handles anything that has to be exact: neighborhood scores, listing scam signals, crew matching, Journeys routes.
- **"What if the model is down?"** Every AI feature falls back to plain code, and there's a global switch to turn AI off.
- **"Why inside Facebook?"** The neighborhoods' Groups, Events, Marketplace and your network are already there.
- **"Privacy?"** No live location, matching is opt-in and never uses protected traits, and you can see and edit everything Muse knows about you.
