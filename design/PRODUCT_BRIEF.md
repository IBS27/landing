# Landing: product brief

An end-to-end apartment solution: find a place, move in, settle in and move out.

## Your City Profile (the base for everything)
- Built from your brief: interests, schedule, budget, no car, "want to meet people".
- Learns from your activity: what you RSVP'd to, joined, liked, skipped and pinned.
- You can see and edit everything Muse thinks it knows about you.

## 1. Find an apartment
- Apartment search tuned to your profile.
- Safety and scam protection: verified listings, plus vouching from your network.
- Unit details: views (sunrise or sunset, ocean), noise risk (nearby colleges, frats, nightlife).

## 2. Neighborhood scores and "Try the neighborhood"
- Ask things like "What's my Saturday like in Fremont vs Capitol Hill?"
- Ember reads local Group threads, and Spark finds what locals agree on.
- Each neighborhood gets a cited Weekend Route on a map ("15 comments, 2 weeks ago").
- Separate scores for things to do, food, transit and fit with your interests, each with its sources.
- Safety: cited facts only (lighting, transit at night, what locals say). No single "safety score".
- Shows the Groups, Events and crew members in each area.
- Find activities and places that match your interests in potential neighborhoods.

## 3. Roommate matching
- Starts from your network: friends of friends and people your mutual friends vouch for.
- Opt-in lifestyle answers: sleep, guests, cleanliness, budget.
- Compatibility score with the traits you share.
- Never uses protected traits.

## 4. Move in and decorate
- Scan your room, then find matching items on Marketplace.
- Style comes from a "vibe" you enter or from your interests, follows and likes.
- Every item you get is tracked for later resale.
- Muse calls for you: gets quotes from movers and finds discounts.

## 5. Getting settled
- Move-in Crew: opt-in matching with people arriving the same month, in the same neighborhood or at the same employer.
  - Matched on interests, never protected traits.
  - Muse creates the Group, writes the intros ("Maya, Dev and Priya all climb") and proposes a first meetup.
  - Starts before move day, so you know people when you land.
- Journeys (your first 90 days):
  - A new Weekend Route every week, built from real Events and Group plans ("Run Club, Green Lake, 8am Saturday").
  - Crew overlap: "Dev and Priya are going. Join?" Muse drafts the message.
  - Weekly check-ins that adapt to what you actually did ("3 weeks in: 2 events, 1 Group, 2 new friends").
  - Levels: Week 1 "Explore", Month 1 "Regular", Month 3 "Local".
- Turn talk into real plans: Muse spots plans in the crew chat ("we should hike sometime"), suggests a time, place and route, runs a poll, creates the Event and sends day-of reminders.

## 6. Living there
- Shopping: search Marketplace (for example, a desk lamp), negotiate, then place the item in a 3D model of your room.
- Roommate life: a shared house Group for chores, bills and groceries.
  - Muse splits bills and suggests chore rotations, and code checks the math.
  - Plan house dinners or a shared Costco run.
- Rent: payments, and possibly rent management.

## 7. Move out and sublease
- Move out: go through your tracked items, pick what to sell, and Landing creates the listings and negotiates the sales.
- Sublease: create the listing and negotiate, with the same scam protection and vouching as apartment search.

## 8. Your City Map (maybe; private by default)
- Pin places you've been, with photos, videos and notes. Pins are added only after you visit.
- Spark tags your photos and suggests pins and captions. Muse groups pins into moments ("First climbing night").
- AI edits: Vista and Muse Image clean up dark or blurry shots, and SAM 3.1 cuts out people and objects for collages.
- Auto-made recaps and vlogs ("Your first month in Seattle").
- Shared crew pins, but everyone tagged has to approve first.
- Memories in 3D (from Re:Collection): rebuild a night out from video and walk through it again with friends.

## Principles
- Never uses protected traits for matching.
- No live location, ever.
- You approve before Muse sends anything.
- Sources are cited for every score and recommendation.
- Core flows still work without AI.
